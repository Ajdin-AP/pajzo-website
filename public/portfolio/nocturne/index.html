Room III — Curator's Notes

“This is what silence looks like at 350 km/h.”

— the curator, on Basalt Longtail

Cast, not stamped

Every NOCTURNE body begins as one continuous side-profile spline, drawn by hand and argued over for months. The panel is then formed as a single piece. There are no seams to hide because there are no seams.

The finish is the argument

Five lacquers, mixed in-house, applied in nine coats and cut back by hand between each. Clearcoat is polished until the reflection of a spotlight holds a straight edge. Then it is polished again.

Please do not touch

Except this one, at 300 km/h. A work that cannot be driven hard is a paperweight with provenance; each of ours is conserved precisely so that it can be used without apology.
