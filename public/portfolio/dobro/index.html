Everything. Shelved properly.

The big stores solved having everything and gave up on finding anything. Dobro keeps one price, one stock figure and one delivery date per item, and puts them where you can read them.

  • 01

    The price is the price

    What you see includes VAT and delivery. Nothing appears at checkout that was not on the shelf.

  • 02

    A date, not a window

    Every item carries the day it lands, worked out from today and the stock actually on the floor.

  • 03

    Stock you can trust

    Units, counted. When something is down to its last few we say so rather than inventing urgency.
